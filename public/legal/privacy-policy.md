# C-Shopper — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]
**Company:** [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("C-Shopper," "we," "us," "our")
**Contact:** [CONTACT EMAIL] (privacy inquiries: [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL, if different])

This Policy explains what data C-Shopper collects, how we use it, and your rights. The App is a free warehouse price-tracker with receipt scanning, watchlists, price alerts, Community Group chat, private Family Groups, and opt-in sharing of receipt items to a public price database.

---

## 1. Data We Collect

### a. Account data
- Name and email address you provide at signup.
- Login/authentication data needed to keep your account secure.

### b. Receipts you scan
- When you scan a receipt with your camera, the extracted item names, prices, store, and date are stored so you can use the catalog, watchlists, and alerts.
- **Raw receipt images stay on your device by default** and are not uploaded to our servers.

### c. Shared price data (only with your explicit consent)
- If you choose to share receipt items to the public price database, you see a **per-item consent screen** before anything is shared.
- Before sharing, the App **strips sensitive data on your device**: full payment card numbers, authorization codes, member/loyalty IDs, and other PII are removed locally. **Only redacted items and prices leave your phone** — the raw, unredacted receipt is never transmitted for public sharing.

### d. Messages and group activity
- Chat messages you send in Community Groups and Family Groups.
- Shared shopping-list items in Family Groups.
- Group membership, mute/report actions you take.

### e. Device and usage data
- Device type/OS, app version, crash logs, and basic usage analytics (e.g., which features you use) to keep the App running and improve it.
- Approximate location is **not** collected at launch; if a future feature needs it (e.g., store-specific alerts), we will ask first and update this Policy.

### f. Reports and moderation records
- Reports you file and moderation actions on your account (needed to enforce the Community Guidelines).

## 2. What We NEVER Collect or Share

- We **never** collect, store, or transmit **full payment card numbers** or **authorization codes**. The on-device stripping described above removes them before any sharing occurs.
- We **never** upload your raw, unredacted receipt images for public sharing.
- We **do not sell your personal data** — not to data brokers, advertisers, or anyone else. We do not share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

- To operate the App: your catalog, watchlists, alerts, groups, and shared price database.
- To moderate content: automated filters attempt to block payment/personal data in public chat; human review handles reported content.
- To keep accounts secure and prevent abuse.
- To improve the App (aggregated, de-identified analytics).
- To communicate with you about the App (e.g., policy updates).
