# C-Shopper — Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]
**Company:** [COMPANY LEGAL NAME]
**Contact / appeals:** [CONTACT EMAIL]

C-Shopper's Community Groups are where warehouse shoppers swap deals, flag price drops, and help each other save money. These guidelines keep that space useful and safe. They apply to Community Groups (public chat, up to 100 members each), Family Groups, the public price database, and anything else you share in the App. Breaking them can get your content removed or your account restricted — see "What happens if you break the rules" below.

---

## ✅ What's encouraged

- **Sharing real deals and price drops** you've seen at warehouse stores — store name, item, price, and date help everyone.
- **Asking and answering** shopping questions ("Anyone seen Kirkland batteries back in stock?").
- **Posting accurate price sightings** to the public price database — double-check the item and price before you share.
- **Being kind and helpful**, especially to new members.
- **Keeping chat on-topic**: prices, deals, product finds, shopping tips.

## 🚫 What's not allowed

1. **Harassment and hate.** No bullying, threats, insults, or targeting anyone. No hate speech or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, gender, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any protected characteristic.
2. **Sharing personal or payment info.** Never post credit/debit card numbers, bank details, passwords, authorization codes, member IDs, phone numbers, home addresses — yours or anyone else's — in public chat or the price database. Our automated filters try to block payment data, but they aren't perfect: **you are responsible for what you post.** Share deal details, not personal details.
3. **Spam and scams.** No repetitive messages, ads for your business, referral-code farming, phishing links, fake "deals" that lead to scam sites, or impersonating staff or other users.
4. **Fake or manipulated prices.** Don't submit prices you didn't actually see, edited receipt data, or joke entries to the price database. Crowdsourced prices only work if they're honest.
5. **Illegal content.** No content that breaks the law — including fraud, theft, counterfeit goods, or instructions for wrongdoing.
6. **Sexual or graphic content.** Keep it family-friendly; groups include teenagers (13+) and family shopping lists.
7. **Evading enforcement.** Don't create new accounts to dodge a mute or ban.

## 📣 How reporting works

- **To report a message or user:** tap the message (or the user's profile) → **Report** → pick a reason → add details if you want. You can also report suspicious price entries.
- Reports go to a **review queue** handled by our moderation team. We aim to review reports within [TIMEFRAME, e.g., 48 hours].
- **You can also mute** any user yourself so you stop seeing their messages — muting is private and instant.
- Filing false or abusive reports is itself a violation.

## ⚖️ What happens if you break the rules

We use a stepped approach, but we may skip steps for serious violations:

1. **Warning** — we tell you what broke the rules.
2. **Content removal** — the message or price entry is taken down.
3. **Mute** — you temporarily can't post in groups ([DURATION, e.g., 24 hours to 7 days]).
4. **Removal from a group** — for repeated disruption in a specific group.
5. **Account suspension or permanent ban** — for repeated violations, or immediately for severe ones (harassment, scams, posting someone's personal data, illegal content).

**Appeals:** if you think we got it wrong, email [CONTACT EMAIL] with your username and what happened. We'll take another look.

## 👨‍👩‍👧 A note on Family Groups and teens

- Family Groups are private to the people you invite, with shared shopping lists — still keep them respectful.
- The App is **13+ only**; there are no accounts for children under 13. Parents: you manage Family Groups on your own account.
- Teens (13–17) should use the App with a parent's or guardian's permission.

## Changes

We may update these guidelines as the community grows; we'll note the effective date above and flag material changes in the App.
